# Clarity R2R

we place recruitment leadership

Clarity R2R is a recruiter-to-recruiter search firm placing leadership into US recruitment
agencies. Everything it sends, from candidate frontsheets to client decks, is gold on
near-black, set in Poppins, and written in plain, numbers-first language.

## Content fundamentals

**Voice.** Clarity introduces clients to candidates and candidates to clients. Write as Clarity:
"Founder and CEO Alex Woodhead spent six years as a Director at Cubiq", never the client's own
first person ("we know exactly what it takes"). Lead with numbers. No recruiter-speak: no
"results-driven", "proven track record", "passionate about".

**Punctuation.** No em dashes, anywhere. Use commas, full stops or semicolons.

**Spelling and money.** British spelling in prose is fine. Money and roles follow US market
conventions: `$`, lowercase `k`, US city names.

### Capitalisation

Anything that works as a headline is **headline case**: capitalise the main words, keep the small
joining words lowercase (`a`, `an`, `the`, `and`, `or`, `of`, `in`, `on`, `to`, `at`, `by`, `for`,
`with`, `from`, `per`, `via`), always capitalise the first and last word.

| Element | Example |
|---|---|
| Role titles | `VP of Business Development, Technology` |
| Company descriptors | `PE-Backed Life Sciences Firm`, `Founder-Led SME` |
| Slide and card headings, labels, badges | `Key Achievements`, `Location and Relocation` |

Exceptions:

- **Locations and section banners: ALL CAPS, letter-spaced.** `LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA`
- **Compensation strings: sentence case.** `$300k+ base + bonus + equity`
- **The tagline is always lowercase:** `we place recruitment leadership`
- Body copy and bullets: sentence case.
- Acronyms stay capitals: `PE`, `VC`, `AI`, `GTM`, `RPO`, `MSP`, `US`, `UK`, `VP`, `SVP`, `CRO`, `R2R`.

## Visual foundations

**Colour.** `gold` on `ground` is the brand. `surface` fills cards and tiles, bordered with `line`.
Body copy is `text`; captions and footers are `muted`. Emphasis panels (compensation) use
`gold-surface` with a `gold-line` border. There is no light theme: Clarity artwork is always dark.

**Type.** Poppins throughout at 400, 600 and 700, no substitutes. Headings and labels are `gold`
and bold. On slides nothing is smaller than `slide-caption` (24px on the 1920 canvas).

**Shapes.** Headshots are always circles (`radius-photo`). Cards and stat tiles use
`radius-card`. Values sit in white-outlined pills (`radius-pill`). No accent stripes, no colour
bars under titles, no gradients.

**Icons.** Line icons in `gold`, Lucide style, about the cap height of the text they sit beside.
Used for sectors, check-mark bullets, location pins and card headers.

## Slides

Decks are 16:9, 1920 x 1080, with `space-8` margins. The pattern every client deck follows:

- **Header on every content slide:** the client's logo top-left, the Clarity wordmark top-right,
  both white. The slide title sits below them, top-left, in `slide-title` gold.
- **Cover:** both logos centred side by side with a small white "x" between, the role in
  `slide-display` gold, the location banner in letter-spaced capitals beneath.
- **Contact card, always last:** circular recruiter headshot, the Clarity wordmark, the name in
  gold capitals, phone and email in white.
- **Layouts that repeat:** a founder intro (text left, circular photo right), a team grid of
  circular headshots with gold names, two to four stat tiles over supporting bullets, icon cards,
  gold label plus description lists in one or two columns, check-mark bullet lists, value pills.
- **Speaker notes are visible to anyone who opens a deck.** Never put sources or internal notes
  there.

## Logos

The Clarity wordmark (`assets/Logos/clarity-r2r-wordmark.png`) is white on transparent, for dark
grounds only. Never stretch it, never recolour it, leave clear space either side. Client logos on
Clarity slides are converted to white so they read on `ground`.
