Introduction

Apera is a founder-led specialist staffing business recruiting for Aerospace, Defense, Space, Automotive, Clean Energy and AI & Robotics clients.

Founder and CEO Alex Woodhead spent six years as a Director at Cubiq Recruitment, then co-founded Strativ Group in 2019. He left Strativ in 2023 and launched Apera in January 2024.

Apera runs a full-service model across contract, permanent, executive search and embedded RPO, taking clients from transactional hiring to subscription RPO partnerships.

Alex has relocated to Los Angeles to lead the US build, where Apera’s client base is concentrated.
